Publication standard

  1. Open the actual broker or owner advertisement, not just an indexed snippet or cached copy.
  2. Establish recent, affirmative evidence that the property remains for sale; do not infer availability from a functioning URL.
  3. Cross-check asking price, floor, living area, number of rooms, bathrooms, terrace, lift, costs and location.
  4. Exclude withdrawn, sold, reserved, inaccessible or insufficiently verifiable entries.
  5. Record a dated, auditable source and recheck before every subsequent report.

About the empty inventory
